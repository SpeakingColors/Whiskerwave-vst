## WhiskerWave playable preview

This is the interactive design mockup of WhiskerWave with its full sound engine, running offline in your web browser.
**It is not the VST3 plugin yet.** The plugin comes after the C++ port of this exact design.

**Download:** `WhiskerWave_Preview_<tag>.zip` below. One zip for Linux, macOS and Windows.

Inside: `WhiskerWave.html` (open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox), `WhiskerWave_Preview_Manual.pdf`, `INSTALL.txt`, licences and checksums.

- Works offline, sends nothing anywhere, nothing to install.
- Click once to start audio, then play with the mouse or the computer keyboard (A to L white keys, W to P black keys, Z and X octave).
- Sound has been checked by measurement, not yet by ear. Presets are starting points.
