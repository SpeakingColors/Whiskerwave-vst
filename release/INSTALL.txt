WhiskerWave preview: install and remove
=======================================

This is the playable preview of WhiskerWave: the full design and sound engine running in your web browser.
It is NOT the VST3 plugin yet. The plugin build follows when the C++ port of this same design is done.

Works on Linux, macOS and Windows. Nothing to install. Works offline. Sends nothing anywhere.

Open it
1. Open WhiskerWave.html with Chrome, Edge or Firefox (double-click, or drag it onto a browser window).
2. Click once anywhere: browsers only start audio after a click.
3. Play the on-screen keys, or your computer keyboard:
   A S D F G H J K L are white keys, W E T Y U O P black keys, Z and X move the octave, C and V change velocity.

Your presets
- Presets you save are stored in this browser on this computer (browser local storage), not in a file.
- To keep a copy, use Export in the preset browser and save the text.

Remove it
- Delete WhiskerWave.html and this folder.
- To also remove your saved presets: clear the site data for this file in your browser settings
  (Chrome or Edge: Settings, Privacy, Site data; Firefox: Settings, Privacy, Cookies and Site Data).

Manual: WhiskerWave_Preview_Manual.pdf (every page and every control with its help text).
